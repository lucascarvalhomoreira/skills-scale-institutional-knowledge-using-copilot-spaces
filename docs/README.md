# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme validates and authorizes initiatives through the initiation phase. Teams confirm the business need, define a SMART objective and measurable success criteria, identify stakeholders and champions, outline the initial timeline and resources, and document early risks. Work moves into planning only after the Project One-pager is reviewed, stakeholders align, and team availability is confirmed.

Planning converts approved initiatives into an actionable backlog and delivery plan. Teams break work into shippable increments, estimate scope, define acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, identify dependencies and cross-team integration points, and agree on milestones and a release plan. A prioritized Risk Register with owners, mitigations, impact, and status keeps delivery uncertainty visible.

Execution uses project boards and regular team rhythms to deliver, review, test, and iterate on work. Daily standups focus on progress and blockers, weekly delivery syncs review progress and risks, and demos occur at sprint or milestone boundaries. Developers use small pull requests linked to issues and acceptance criteria; CI runs tests, linting, and security scans, and the team requires review and the appropriate quality checks before merging.

OctoAcme manages stakeholder communication through weekly PM–Product Manager alignment, regular status updates, and milestone-based or ad-hoc escalations. Releases require passing checks, acceptance criteria, release notes, smoke tests, a rollback or mitigation plan, and post-deployment verification. After sprints, releases, incidents, or important milestones, retrospectives capture lessons and convert them into owned, dated improvement actions that are tracked through the backlog and continuous improvement process.
